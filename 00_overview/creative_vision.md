# Creative Vision: The Dual-Track System

## Core Ethos
To bridge the gap between industrial-grade design systems and the messy, human meditations on the environments we inhabit.

## The Tracks
1. **Professional (The Grid):** "Designing systems that make sense." 
   - Visual Language: Precision, transparency, logic.
   - Purpose: Demonstrating ROI, usability, and architectural thinking.
2. **Personal (The Scrapbook / 000 Essays):** "Meditations on the systems we live in."
   - Visual Language: Analog, tactile, irregular.
   - Purpose: Demonstrating curiosity, data-literacy, and philosophical depth.

## Success Metrics
- Recruiter clarity on Senior-level leadership (4+ years).
- Recognition of "Systems Thinking" as a core competency.
- Seamless navigation between high-fidelity UI and low-fidelity essays.
